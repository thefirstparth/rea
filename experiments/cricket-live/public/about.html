About & privacy

What Crease is

Crease shows live scores, ball-by-ball commentary, scorecards and squads for India and the IPL, plus every other match in progress. It is free, has no accounts and no ads, and is an independent project.

Where the cricket comes from

Scores, scorecards, squads and fixtures come from Cricbuzz. Extra match details and the live blog come from ESPNcricinfo. Crease reads them from their public websites; it is not affiliated with Cricbuzz, ESPNcricinfo, the BCCI or the IPL, and they don't endorse it. Their pages can change without notice, so a score may be late or a section may be missing for a while.

Commentary text is theirs. The Cricinfo live blog includes reader comments, shown as the blog shows them.

What Crease stores

Nothing about you. There are no cookies and no analytics. Your choices are kept in your own browser (local storage) and never leave it:

  • crease-clock: 12- or 24-hour time.
  • crease-theme: light or dark, when you pick one.
  • crease-comm: your commentary source.
  • crease-alerts: whether alerts are on.

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes them all.

Alerts

Alerts only work after you allow notifications in your browser. Crease then checks for match starts, wickets, DRS reviews and milestones while it is open, and shows the notification itself. To show notifications on Android, browsers need a small background script (a service worker); Crease installs one only when you turn alerts on, removes it when you turn them off, and it stores nothing.

Other sites your browser contacts

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) for the typefaces. Google receives your IP address, as with any font request.
  • Cricbuzz (static.cricbuzz.com) for flags and player photos.
  • ESPN (a.espncdn.com) for IPL team logos.

Images are requested without telling those sites which page you were on (no referrer). Score data is fetched by Crease's own server, so Cricbuzz and ESPNcricinfo don't see your browser for that.
